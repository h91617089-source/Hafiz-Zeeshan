🚀 Weather Pro 360 - What's New (Update Changelog)

We have added several exciting and smart features in this update to enhance your experience:

1. 🕒 Smart Time Greetings: The app now automatically greets you with "Good Morning," "Good Afternoon," or "Good Evening" based on your current local time.
2. 🌡️ Feels Like Temperature: Added the "Feels Like" metric to give you a more accurate sense of the weather based on wind and humidity.
3. ⚖️ Yesterday vs. Today Comparison: A live comparison telling you exactly how much hotter or colder today is compared to yesterday.
4. ⏳ Sunrise & Sunset Countdown: Real-time countdown showing exactly how many hours and minutes are left until the next sunrise or sunset.
5. 🕰️ Weather Time Machine: The app now reveals what the temperature was in your city exactly 3 years ago on this exact date.
6. 🌙 Moon Phase System: Added detailed moon phase tracking (New Moon, Full Moon, Crescent) along with illumination percentage for night reports.
7. ✂️ Refined Short Format: The "Short Format" setting has been strictly streamlined to output only the Temperature and Weather Condition for a quicker, distraction-free reading.
8. 🗣️ Screen Reader (Jieshuo) Optimization: Removed disruptive emojis from the text output and fixed all content descriptions on buttons to ensure a flawless and fully accessible screen reader experience.
9. 🎤 Voice Search Restored: The microphone-based city voice search has been completely fixed and is fully operational again.
10. 🌐 Multi-Language Support: All newly added features are fully translated and support Roman Urdu, Hindi, and English based on your saved settings.
