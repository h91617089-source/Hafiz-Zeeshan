Weather Pro 360 - New Update Features:

1. Current Location Name Announcement:
   - Now automatically detects and announces exact local town, tehsil, or area names using Android native system maps.

2. Weather Memory Lane & Nostalgia:
   - Enjoy historical weather fun-facts and past climate memories matching today's date for a warm community feel.

3. Swipeable Forecast Lists:
   - 24-hour and multi-day forecasts are now converted into easy swipeable line-by-line lists for screen reader users.

4. Advanced Atmospheric Details:
   - Added live Atmospheric Pressure (hPa), Air Humidity (%), and Visibility Range (km) to the main report.

5. Clean Screen Reader Experience:
   - Removed all distracting equal and dash symbols to ensure a smooth, natural reading flow.
